# ALIGN Health — visual identity draft 0.1

## Brand idea

Three distinct streams represent heterogeneous surveys, populations and measurement instruments. They approach a common reference while remaining visible as separate sources. The open coral node represents evidence entering deliberation and policy, not a closed or predetermined answer.

## Wordmark

**ALIGN Health**  
**Descriptor:** Health survey harmonisation for policy

Use `ALIGN` in bold and `Health` in regular weight. Preferred typeface: Source Sans 3. For long-form academic documents, pair it with Source Serif 4.

## Colour system

| Role | Colour | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific authority | Deep petrol | `#174A5B` |
| Harmonisation | Sage | `#6F9B8E` |
| Secondary source | Light sage | `#91B5AA` |
| Policy/action accent | Muted coral | `#D97762` |
| Background | Cool ivory | `#F5F6F2` |
| Text | Graphite | `#202A2E` |

Coral is an accent only. Do not use it as a large background colour or as decoration without semantic meaning.

## Usage principles

- Preserve clear space around the logo equal to the diameter of the open coral node.
- Use the full horizontal mark above 420 px wide; use the icon below that threshold.
- Prefer the ivory or white background. Use the monochrome version when colour reproduction is unavailable.
- Do not close or fill the coral node, merge the three streams into one band, add shadows, gradients or medical symbols.
- In data graphics, petrol identifies the common framework, sage colours identify sources or survey families, and coral identifies policy-facing outputs or decisions.

## Status

This is a design-development draft. Before public adoption, verify the project name, domain and relevant marks, then test contrast, reduction, print reproduction and stakeholder interpretation.
